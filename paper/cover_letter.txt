I am pleased to submit our original research manuscript entitled:
"FP-Ensemble: Backpropagation-Free Convolutional Learning via Fused Random Projection Forward Targets"
for consideration as a regular paper in IEEE Transactions on Neural Networks and Learning Systems.

Gradient backpropagation, while ubiquitous, suffers from severe sequential computational locking, excessive GPU activation memory storage, and high hardware energy expenditure. To address these fundamental bottlenecks, this paper proposes FP-Ensemble—a closed-form, backpropagation-free convolutional deep learning architecture.

Key novelties and contributions of this manuscript include:
1. Formulation of the FP-Ensemble learning paradigm, utilizing an ensemble of independent Gaussian random projection pairs (Q, U) fused via convex expectation, establishing an analytical Ridge regression layer solve.
2. Formal mathematical proof (Theorem 1) demonstrating that fused random target representations reduce variance with asymptotic rate O(1/R), explaining the optimal bias-variance equilibrium at R=5.
3. Formulation of Conv2D-FP, an unfolded patch regression mechanism (im2col) with mini-batch Gram matrix accumulation that enables deep convolutional architectures to be trained analytically without spatial error backpropagation.
4. Extensive empirical evaluation across four diverse benchmark datasets (Sign Language MNIST, Fashion-MNIST, SVHN, and CIFAR-10), demonstrating consistent accuracy improvements over canonical Forward Projection (+1.3% to +3.8%), up to 18x training speedups over standard backpropagation CNNs, and over 60% reduction in peak GPU VRAM allocation.

This work has not been published previously, is not under consideration for publication elsewhere, and its submission has been approved by the author.

Thank you for your consideration of our work.
